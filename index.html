Overview

Green energy is electricity or heat generated from natural, renewable sources -such as sunlight, wind, water and geothermal heat- that produce little to no greenhouse gases.

Sources of renewable energy

1. solar Energy

Solar PV panels convert sunlight directly into DC electricity, which is then converted into AC electricity via an inverter for use in homes and businesses.

Technologies used
  • Photovoltanics (PV)
  • Solar Thermal
  • Passive Solar

2. Wind Energy

Wind energy is a clean, renewable power source that harnesses the kinetic energy of air in motion using turbines to generate electricity

Technologies Used
  • Harvesting wind
  • Conversion
  • Distribution

3. Geothermal

Geothermal energy is a renewable, low-carbon power source derived from the Earth's internal heat, generated by the planet's formation and radioactive decay.
